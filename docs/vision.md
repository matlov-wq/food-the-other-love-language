# Vision & Philosophy

## Core vision

A cookbook celebrating food as a love language: chosen family, intention,
care, nostalgia, and accessibility for ADHD cooks. Recipes that invite people
to gather.

## Opening quote

> "Love isn't grand gestures. It's attention, repetition, and presence."

## Tone

Tone should emerge organically, recipe by recipe — not be forced into a
house style. It's okay for some recipes to be funny and some to be tender.
What ties them together is care, not a consistent voice.

## Book categories

- Starters
- Sides
- Sauces & Condiments
- Entrees
- Desserts
- Party Snacks
- Non-Alcoholic Beverages
- Mocktails
- Boozy Drinks

(Sweet Potato Soufflé belongs in **Sides**, not Desserts — decided early on,
worth keeping consistent.)

## Key format decisions

- Recipes repeat ingredient measurements *in the instructions*, not just the
  ingredient list — this is core to the ADHD-friendly format (see
  `recipe-standards.md`).
- Optional/bonus notes are labeled **"If You Feel Like It"** rather than
  "Optional" or "Tip" — it's part of the book's voice.
- Batch versions of drink recipes are included where relevant.
- Benny's Holiday Tea should explain the individual tea blend components so
  readers in other regions can substitute.

## Collaboration

Collaboration with Benny is organic, with no predefined roles. Worth
revisiting once the app is real — shared editing, attribution, and who-owns
what get more concrete once there's a live product and not just a doc.

## Publishing vision (long-term, not MVP)

Digital-first launch → premium spiral-bound physical edition → broader
online retail. Not a blocker for building the app — noted here so it isn't
lost.
